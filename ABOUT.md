# About CMe CheerTsuMe'

CMe CheerTsuMe' is the product identity for the CMe Enterprise Platform: a commerce and ERP operating system built on the ZEAZ engineering baseline.

## Product focus

- ERP and commerce operations
- inventory, procurement, manufacturing, quality and fulfillment
- accounting, CRM, billing and enterprise analytics
- AI-assisted content workflows and localization
- official commerce/social platform integrations
- automation, observability, security and recovery

## Engineering principles

CMe is secure by default, tenant-aware, automation-first, testable, observable, documented and evidence-driven. Security and quality failures are fixed rather than bypassed.

## Repository

- Project: CMe CheerTsuMe' Enterprise Platform
- Repository: cvsz/cme
- Governance baseline: ZEAZ engineering controls

Credentials, private account data and production secrets must never be committed.
